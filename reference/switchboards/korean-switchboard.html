Korean Sentence Switchboard

One sentence, six switches. Flip a switch and only the pieces it affects change, outlined in red. Tap any piece to see why it looks the way it does.

What changed

Prototype. Hangul with Revised Romanization (as pronounced) or IPA. The IPA shows each word as said on its own, including sound changes inside the word. Every switch combination was generated and checked.
